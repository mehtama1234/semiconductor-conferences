# End-to-End Goal

Build a dated, evidence-backed intelligence system for ISCA, DAC, and ICCAD that collects paper metadata and permitted abstracts, classifies each paper into themes, subthemes, and sub-subthemes, and publishes deep pages explaining where semiconductor and computer-system research is headed.

## Required analysis

- identify the technical problem, method, artifact, workload, hardware/process assumptions, baselines, and metrics for each paper;
- distinguish direct paper evidence from synthesis and inference;
- compare conference editions and refuse rising/declining claims without comparable snapshots;
- explain convergence and divergence across architecture, EDA, physical design, packaging, reliability, security, and AI-for-design;
- preserve limitations, negative results, missing data, access constraints, and classification disagreements.

## Required output

Each conference folder must eventually contain normalized paper data, a provenance ledger, theme/subtheme/sub-subtheme reports, and deep pages. The root must contain the shared taxonomy, cross-conference matrices, temporal comparison, and final synthesis.
