## How to read the analysis

Every page separates direct evidence from synthesis and inference. A title or CFP
topic is a discovery signal; a description or abstract supports a technical claim;
an adjudicated record must preserve baselines, metrics, and limitations. None of
the current pages is a market forecast or an industrial-adoption claim.
