## Priority rules

Order: synthesis anchors, secondary-source recovery, unresolved ISCA identifiers, other coded records, captured descriptions, remaining metadata. Ties use conference, edition, then paper ID. Each JSONL row retains separate collection, coding, source depth, and review states.

## Limits

- Priority is review routing, not importance or prevalence.
- Inventory title counts include student and invited entries; explicit nonregular flags must be respected in regular-paper comparisons, and untyped entries remain unaudited.
- Source tier does not establish full-text extraction or independent acceptance.
- Historical challenge/rate-limit outcomes are preserved; metadata-only does not mean inaccessible.
- Independent review remains pending; same-model preliminary review is separate.
- Coverage is selected for recoverability and diversity; unidentified counterexamples and thin taxonomy branches still need manual prioritization.
