# Directional challenge batch — 2026-09-06

Six additional DAC 2026 cases qualify the [six hypotheses](field-directions-and-hypotheses-v1.md).
These are purposively selected official descriptions, not full-paper adjudication,
independent review, or a representative sample. The [ledger](../data/direction-challenges-v1.json)
records paper IDs, source-text hashes, interpretations and next evidence checks.
Original URLs and text remain in the referenced draft review packets.

| Direction | Additional case | What changes in the interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Verified AI design | NotSoTiny (2760) | Verification tools alone do not establish capability on difficult, uncontaminated tasks. Benchmark construction is part of the evidence. |
| Memory-centric execution | TierANNS / COGA (2268) | Layout, queues and speculation offer a route distinct from compute offload. Resolve the title/body naming mismatch before deeper attribution. |
| Reliability and security | AvaTEE (1494) | Confidentiality and integrity leave availability open; scheduling and resource commitments enlarge the relevant security contract. |
| Physical integration | Thermal stability strategy (1862) | Thermal effects couple to stored weights and output accuracy, not merely cooling and throughput. Calibration has costs and residual error. |
| Fidelity-aware exploration | ChiSER (399) | Rechecking selected winners can still miss good candidates filtered out by an inaccurate surrogate; a reference model is not silicon. |
| Specialized acceleration | OpenACMv2 (2014) | Approximation must respect task accuracy and process variation, not just nominal arithmetic or PPA gains. |

The common refinement is that constraints need to govern **selection and evaluation**,
not merely appear in an optimization objective. However, this is our interpretation
of selected mechanisms. None of these descriptions establishes that the field as
a whole is moving faster in that direction, nor that the proposed systems succeed
outside their evaluated conditions.

Next: recover primary depth for these six cases, prioritizing NotSoTiny's evaluation
challenge and TierANNS/COGA's identity discrepancy. Broaden conference coverage;
do not treat another DAC sample as a substitute for ISCA and ICCAD evidence.
