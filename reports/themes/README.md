## Reading rule

The record totals come from the deduplicated adjudicated ledgers under `*/data/adjudicated-*.jsonl`, a selected coded sample spanning three venues. They are not prevalence, ranking, or trend estimates. Follow every material claim to the linked source and then to the condition and review registers.
