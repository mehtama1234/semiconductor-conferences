## HBM-CASO

HBM-CASO is an exact ISCA 2026 program title with DOI metadata. The recovered
indexed summary describes coordinated system-level and on-die ECC, including a
stronger on-die codeword and delayed verification that accumulates parity over a
batch of writes. It claims broader error-pattern correction with minimal
performance and bandwidth overhead.

The paper is especially relevant to the ECC cluster formed by Cerberus and the
ECC-enabled PIM record. However, the current project has not recovered an author
preprint, publisher abstract, or full text. The claim therefore remains a
secondary-index discovery signal. [Indexed summary](https://eurekamag.com/research/109/525/109525273.php)
