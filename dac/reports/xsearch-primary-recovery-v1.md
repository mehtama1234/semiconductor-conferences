# XSearch evidence-depth assessment — 2026-09-06

The [official description](https://63dac.conference-program.com/presentation/?id=RESEARCH1186&sess=sess167)
identifies multi-fidelity Bayesian exploration and calibration across simulators,
applied to XiangShan. It reports no numerical outcomes or evaluated baselines.
The existing coding's single-fidelity comparator labels are researcher-inferred
comparison ideas, not experiments documented by this source.

This is a useful mechanism example for fidelity-aware search, not evidence that
the method beats a particular optimizer. Simulator identities, search objective,
parameter ranges, calibration errors and total evaluation budgets are unknown.
Final-design feasibility and held-out high-fidelity evaluation matter more than
the number of cheap design points explored. Simulator calibration also does not
establish silicon accuracy.

Recorded searches recovered no exact manuscript or implementation. Overlapping
authors on BigPower do not make that different work a substitute. Using an
open-source processor is not evidence that XSearch itself has been released.

The [structured assessment](../data/xsearch-primary-recovery-v1.json) preserves
the original coding and clarifies its interpretation. Independent review remains
pending; absent numbers must not be treated as zero improvement.
