# CANNON source-depth assessment — 2026-09-06

The [official description](https://63dac.conference-program.com/presentation/?id=RESEARCH108&sess=sess119)
and [author-lab publication entry](https://bdai.yonsei.ac.kr/conferences.html)
match the title and nine authors. The lab's Paper link targets the conference
homepage, not a manuscript; no implementation link appears in that entry.

The official performance headline and the
[university announcement](https://ai.yonsei.ac.kr/csai/board/resultBoard.do?articleNo=466612&mode=view)
differ: up to two orders of magnitude versus over 800 times. Neither provides
the per-case conditions needed to reconcile them. Preserve both attributions;
do not promote the larger number into quantitative synthesis.

The mechanism remains a reported real-CXL implementation of pipelined ANN
offload. This does not establish independent replication or end-to-end RAG gains.
Matched recall, memory-residency regimes, baseline tuning, device capacities and
absolute latency/throughput are essential to separate capacity effects from
execution acceleration. UPVSS remains a qualitative mechanism comparison, not a
matched performance baseline or evidence that one platform replaces another.

The [structured assessment](../data/cannon-primary-recovery-v1.json) records
bounded discovery and missing conditions. No full paper was recovered in these
checks; this is not a claim of inaccessibility. Independent review stays pending.
