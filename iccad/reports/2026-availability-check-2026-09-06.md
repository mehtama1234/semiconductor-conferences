# ICCAD 2026 availability check — initial check 2026-09-06; rechecked 2026-09-07

The official ICCAD 2026 site was checked on 2026-09-06 and rechecked on
2026-09-07. The site identifies the
conference as November 8–12, 2026 and exposes the call for papers, author
instructions, keynotes, workshops, special sessions, panels, and tutorials. It
still does not expose a regular-paper program, paper-title inventory, or proceedings
corpus that can replace the 2025 fallback.

The official FAQ states that the program will be updated as it is confirmed and
that the program pages should be used once published. The current Program menu
does not contain a regular-paper inventory. Proceedings access is described as
available to registered participants at the start of the event.

## Research consequence

`iccad/data/papers-2025-program.jsonl` and its adjudicated evidence remain the
explicit fallback for the 2026 target. This check does not claim that ICCAD
2026 has no accepted papers; it records only that a public paper corpus was not available through the official site at the check date.

Next check: revisit the official Program menu and proceedings/publication
channels after publication. If a 2026 inventory appears, collect it as a new
edition and preserve this fallback rather than overwriting it.
