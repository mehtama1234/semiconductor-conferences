# ICCAD — International Conference on Computer-Aided Design

Focus: computer-aided design algorithms and systems, synthesis, verification, optimization, physical design, analog/mixed-signal design, security, and emerging design methods.

2026 is the target edition for conference scope and direction. If the 2026 paper corpus is not publicly accessible, ICCAD 2025 is the paper-analysis fallback and will be labeled separately from 2026 metadata.

Initial authoritative sources:

- [ICCAD 2026 official home](https://iccad.com/2026)
- [ICCAD 2026 Call for Papers](https://iccad.com/2026/authors/call-for-papers)
- [ICCAD 2025 full program — fallback paper corpus](https://2025.iccad.com/full-program/)
- [ICCAD 2025 program PDF — fallback paper corpus](https://confcats-event-sessions.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/iccad25/uploads/ICCAD_2025_Program_v8.pdf)

Subfolders: `data/raw/` for permitted source responses, `reports/` for ledgers and analyses, and `pages/` for deep topic pages.
