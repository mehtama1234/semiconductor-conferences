directions

Six directions to watch

Each with its evidence, its counter-evidence and its test.

directions/01 constraints become executable

Requirements checked while designing

A candidate is useful only when executable constraints can reject, repair, or rank it.

directions/02 data movement as contract

Moving data is the real cost

Useful computation is constrained by where data is, how far it moves, and what competes for the path.

directions/03 closed loop design

Measure, then decide the next design

A design loop is only useful when sensing cost, model error, decision latency (the wait for one result), and update semantics are explicit.

directions/04 reliability security upstream

Plan for faults and attacks early

Performance without a declared fault and threat operating envelope is not a complete system result.

directions/05 physical reality in the loop

Heat, wires and power in the loop

An abstract optimum is irrelevant when the physical system cannot realize it.

directions/06 evaluation as research

Better ways to measure results

Every result is a measurement with a task population, evaluator, result, uncertainty, and failure boundary.
