Themes

Ten themes, each explaining one technical problem before its papers

Start with any theme; each ends with links to its subthemes and their papers.

Ten broad subjects. Each article explains the basic technical problem first, then links to its subthemes and their papers.

AI-assisted EDA

59 papers in 8 subthemes. The point: a model may propose a design, but only a tool check makes it count.

Analog, mixed-signal, and emerging devices

38 papers in 15 subthemes. The point: when physics does the arithmetic, noise and drift set the accuracy.

Architecture and accelerators

103 papers in 12 subthemes. The point: more arithmetic units help only when data arrives in time.

Chiplets, 3D integration, and packaging

25 papers in 9 subthemes. The point: splitting a chip into pieces trades cost for heat and wiring between dies.

Design methodology and tooling

29 papers in 16 subthemes. The point: how a design is written down decides which choices a tool can see.

Memory, interconnect, and storage

85 papers in 9 subthemes. The point: moving data, not computing it, often sets the time and energy.

Physical design and manufacturing

42 papers in 13 subthemes. The point: placement, wiring, timing and power all push on each other.

Power, reliability, and security

78 papers in 15 subthemes. The point: protection has a cost, so the fault or attack must be named first.

Systems, applications, and workloads

71 papers in 14 subthemes. The point: judge a system by the useful work users get, not average speed.

Verification and validation

52 papers in 9 subthemes. The point: a check proves only what its stated property and tests cover.
