CANNON: what reading the full source showed

The official description and author-lab publication entry match the title and nine authors. The lab's Paper link targets the conference homepage, not a manuscript; no implementation link appears in that entry.

The official performance headline and the university announcement differ: up to two orders of magnitude versus over 800 times. Neither provides the per-case conditions needed to reconcile them. Preserve both attributions; do not promote the larger number into quantitative synthesis.

The mechanism remains a reported real-CXL implementation of pipelined ANN offload. This does not establish independent replication or end-to-end RAG gains.

Matched recall, memory-residency regimes, baseline tuning, device capacities and absolute latency/throughput are essential to separate capacity effects from execution acceleration. UPVSS remains a qualitative mechanism comparison, not a matched performance baseline or evidence that one platform replaces another.

The structured assessment records bounded discovery and missing conditions. No full paper was recovered in these checks; this is not a claim of inaccessibility. Independent review stays pending.
