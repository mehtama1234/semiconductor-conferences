The official presentation matches the inventory title and all four authors. It describes a steady-state analytical thermal solver. Its approximate 2,000-slice comparison is within one DeepOHeat inference-time budget, not a slices-per-second rate. The error norm, output resolution and inference (running a trained AI model to get answers) duration remain unspecified.

The Fuzhou University announcement identifies the same work but adds no reproducible benchmark configuration. It is institutional reporting, not independent validation. Neither source establishes transient accuracy or measured-silicon agreement.

No exact full paper or verified implementation was recovered in the recorded searches.
