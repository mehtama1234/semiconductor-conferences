FLASH3D: what reading the full source showed

The official presentation matches the inventory title and all four authors. It describes a steady-state analytical thermal solver. Its approximate 2,000-slice comparison is within one DeepOHeat inference-time budget, not a slices-per-second rate. The error norm, output resolution and inference duration remain unspecified.

The Fuzhou University announcement identifies the same work but adds no reproducible benchmark configuration. It is institutional reporting, not independent validation. Neither source establishes transient accuracy or measured-silicon agreement.

No exact full paper or verified implementation was recovered in the recorded searches. Preserve the original description, but withhold quantitative synthesis pending matched geometry/material/boundary conditions, solver tolerances, hardware and runtime accounting. Search non-recovery is not an inaccessibility claim. Same-name computer-vision projects are excluded.

The structured assessment records attempts, source locators, missing conditions and a source-link revisit trigger. It accompanies the review packet as researcher interpretation; independent review remains pending.
