The official description matches the CityUHK institutional record in title and all nine authors. The institutional access link returns to the description; no full paper or verified implementation was recovered in these checks.

Keep three populations separate: training design points, predicted configurations, and fabricated macros. The description reports five macros, not fabrication of the training corpus or the entire predicted design space. How training labels were obtained and how surrogate (a fast model that stands in for a slow simulation) error was validated remain unknown.

The large FoM ratio lacks its formula and normalization. It cannot be used as a throughput (how much work is finished per second) speedup.

Best-macro and cross-level results are distinct comparisons; precision, operating point, peripheral costs and matched specifications are required before numerical synthesis. Silicon testing reported in an abstract does not establish production yield or model accuracy throughout the search space.

The structured assessment preserves reported values, unknown conditions and a source-release revisit trigger. Independent review remains pending; source non-recovery is not inaccessibility.
