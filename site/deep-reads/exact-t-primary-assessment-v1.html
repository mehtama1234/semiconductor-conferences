The version-pinned preprint matches the program title and all four authors. Structured assessment records experimental conditions and the unresolved description/preprint metric difference. Original description evidence is preserved, not overwritten.

The synthesis may keep this as a provisional example of mapping for one resource. It is not an accepted headline number, nor evidence of learned search. Neither local library optimality nor software tests establish globally optimal circuits.

Figure and artifact follow-up

Visual inspection of PDF page 6 separates the mapper variants and records a modeled space-time counterexample in the structured assessment. Nearby prose and chart attribution differ; the figure check does not resolve the conference-description discrepancy.

The pinned repository contains implementation and benchmark files. Its verification script requires Slurm despite the README's local-looking invocation.

Inspection also shows that the simulator does not test relative phases between basis states, leaving a coherent-oracle correctness gap. These are same-agent observations, not an independent verdict.

Next: reconcile source claims and check both functional and phase correctness before attempting accepted benchmark reproduction.

The pinned revision compiled with GNU C++ 11.4.0/CMake 3.22.1 on Linux x86_64. No simulator, equivalence check or paper benchmark was run.
