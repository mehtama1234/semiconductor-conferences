The official description and first-author page match title and nine authors. Checked author pages and the five-entry public repository metadata listing did not yield a full paper or implementation. This is bounded non-recovery, not proof of inaccessibility.

FuseDot groups repeated-weight multiplication work and introduces reordering and multiplier sharing. The key unresolved question is the net cost under actual quantization (storing numbers with fewer bits) and workload conditions.

As a researcher inference (running a trained AI model to get answers), equal integer codes need not represent equal effective weights across differently scaled groups. Full-paper legality rules and finite-precision accuracy checks are needed.

Weight repetition, index/reordering overhead and contention also need accounting.

Keep reported energy-efficiency factors separate from energy-reduction percentages. The description does not identify the baseline (the existing method a new result is compared against) accelerators or pair each range endpoint with a model/configuration.

Numerical synthesis remains withheld. The author page mentions an award.

It is from a HUST annual conference, not DAC, and it is not an independent review of this assessment.

The structured recovery record lists attempts, interpretation limits and revisit conditions. Review is pending.
