Deep read · a paper checked against its full source

RAF: what reading the full source showed

The official description matches the program title and seven authors. It separates remote-access removal from local-access minimization, first within operators and then between them. That distinction matters: remote-access-free does not mean zero data movement.

The description explicitly pairs results with baselines in order:

Baseline Reported energy reduction Reported speedup
IANUS 50.4% 3.98×
H^2LLM 39.0% 1.72×
OptiPIM 37.8% 1.57×

This resolves baseline attribution, not workload conditions. The communication cost definition, allowed operations, topology, capacities and proof assumptions are absent. Consequently, claimed communication bounds cannot be independently verified from the description or generalized to energy/latency optimality.

No exact full paper or artifact was recovered in the recorded searches. The structured assessment preserves these limits and missing evaluation conditions; numerical synthesis and independent acceptance remain pending.
