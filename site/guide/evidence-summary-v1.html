Field completeness

Which record fields are filled in, and which are missing

568 of 582 records have all required technical fields populated. Missing-field counts are in the JSON file. An empty field means the evidence did not show the property. It does not mean the paper lacks it.

Limits

What this evidence summary cannot show

  • Official descriptions and abstracts compress experimental conditions; numerical comparisons still require paper/table review.
  • The adjudication sample is selected for recoverable evidence and diversity, so theme counts cannot be read as venue-wide frequencies.
  • The deterministic preflight does not replace independent adversarial LLM (large language model: an AI model that reads and writes text) or human judgment.
